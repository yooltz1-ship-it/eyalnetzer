TÈKÈS

A composition by Eyal Netzer for saxophone and string ensemble.

About the Work

Tèkès is a 2025 composition by Eyal Netzer for saxophone and string ensemble. Since its release, it has received strong critical reception and radio broadcast in Israel and abroad. The piece is built to be modular — scalable from a full string ensemble down to smaller chamber configurations with piano, synth, and percussion — and has been performed to sold-out audiences in multiple formats since early 2026.
