About the Project

Falling Into Self is Eyal Netzer's solo project, built around saxophone and winds layered with live electronics. Stripped of a band context, the project leans fully into improvisation and texture — loops, extended technique, and electronic processing built in real time around a single performer.

The project's debut recording, Falling Into Self (Pool Session), was released in 2024.
